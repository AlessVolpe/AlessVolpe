# Alessandro Volpe

Backend software engineer, computer engineering student, compulsive side-project starter (I am a creative mess), and enthusiastically unspecialized individual.

I mostly work with **Python** and **C#/.NET**, with a particular interest in backend systems, APIs, cloud infrastructure, and the occasional project that grows far beyond the scope it was originally supposed to have.

I've worked on production software involving **AI applications and other software systems in the automotive and public sectors**, as well as containerized applications using **Docker and Kubernetes**. Outside work, I use personal and open-source projects as an excuse to explore technologies, software design, and ideas I don't necessarily get to touch professionally.

Currently based in **Rome**, but open to relocating elsewhere in the EU, with a particular fondness for Lithuania after living there for three years.

## Things I spend too much time thinking about

- **Computer science:** software architecture, cybersecurity, algorithms, theoretical computer science, and why abstractions inevitably come back to demand payment.
- **Politics & philosophy:** political theory, philosophy of technology, political economy, and the uncomfortable relationship between technology and the society that produces it.
- **Books:** fiction, philosophy, politics, and whatever else ends up multiplying on my shelves faster than I can read it.
- **Music:** guitar, bass, piano, composition, and music production. The less technical half of my work lives outside GitHub.
- **Open source:** both as a way of learning and because software becomes considerably more interesting once other people can inspect your mistakes.
